Orientation / PokéAgent Challenge

The research gym

Article · Experiment-first wikiEdit source ↗

This is a working notebook for climbing the PokéAgent Challenge through reproducible experiments. Start with one released policy, understand its failures, and test one change at a time. Learn through videos and things you can manipulate in a browser.

Status: unrun. There are no trained policies, baseline results, or performance claims in this repository. Local gizmos use synthetic examples. They require no GPU.

The summit

The goal is a reproducible improvement in Gen 1 OU under the standard timer, followed by a technical report and official-server battle logs. The comparison has two parts: improve over the unchanged starting checkpoint on held-out teams and opponents, then challenge a strong published Metamon rival chosen before training.

The official challenge is an ongoing benchmark. Its battling guide describes 30 released RL baselines and a standard maximum of 8 seconds per turn. Those facts describe the benchmark, not progress in this repository. Rules were checked on 2026-10-24 and must be checked again before a run.

Begin with a reproduction

Read reproduce the first baseline. The first deliverable is a pinned protocol and a budget, followed by a separately authorized baseline run. No model download or training is part of this scaffold.

The proposed starting policy is Minikazam. The proposed rival is Kakuna. Resolve both to immutable artifacts before freezing the protocol. The learner chooses the first hypothesis. An agent can help build the run plumbing without supplying a conclusion.

Follow the evidence

  1. Read a battle as an RL loop. Predict what the policy can and cannot observe.
  2. Freeze a fair evaluation. Make the comparison difficult to move after seeing results.
  3. Try sampling uncertainty. See how little a short winning streak establishes.
  4. Change the opponent pool. Watch a synthetic ranking reverse.
  5. Improve one trained policy. Change one mechanism and keep the starting checkpoint intact.
  6. Publish a reproducible result. Separate measured evidence from interpretation.

Watch before reading more

Watch, play, try pairs each online resource with a small action. Start with the Metamon Gen 1 demonstration, then play with an RL gridworld. External resources require internet. No media or game artwork is copied here.

Keep it yours

Write your prediction before moving a slider or viewing a result. Keep your original wording when an agent suggests edits. A failed prediction is useful evidence about your mental model.
